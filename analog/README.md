## Analog

<img width="1298" height="767" alt="Image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/03dcf234-f099-4782-8f9c-875c695e7cdf" />

- [ntsc-td](https://github.com/olegchomp/ntsc-td) — NTSC composite and VHS. Encoding, head switching, and ringing.
- [td-modulation](analog/td-modulation) — grayscale scanline effect. Brightness shifts the phase of a cosine carrier.
- [td-crt](analog/td-crt) — draws the input as a signal on a CRT tube. The shader runs on a GLSL TOP. Controls live on the parent CRT page.
